name: independent-review
description: Review or hand off an exact revision independently against acceptance criteria, risks and governance.
---
Read ../../MANIFEST.md and G1. Identify implementer and reviewer; the same agent cannot independently review its own work. Provide exact commit, diff, relevant dependencies, tests and limitations. Reviewer checks acceptance, regression, failure paths, privacy and manifest compliance; report blocking findings with evidence and distinguish suggestions. Recheck affected fixes. Mark review pending until actually performed. Owner decides approval; reviewer does not grant deployment.
