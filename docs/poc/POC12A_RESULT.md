## Design implications (proposals, not changes)
Use progress and current-room for observation/presentation, never alone for completion, retained-task identity or readiness. Correlate independent lifecycle/record evidence; do not require 100 percent. Distinguish cleaning, return, dock and maintenance; determine readiness with independently verified criteria, not a charging sample or invented dwell threshold. Preserve all approved mapping, freshness, policy and uncertainty gates. Preserve raw map names exactly including whitespace; segment normalization only as approved.
